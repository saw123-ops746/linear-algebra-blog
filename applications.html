Where linear algebra is used

Vectors and matrices help describe and solve problems in technology, science, and everyday life.

Graphics

Games use vectors for points and matrices to rotate, resize, and move objects.

Data and machine learning

Tables become matrices. Models combine inputs and weights with matrix multiplication; dot products compare vectors.

Science and engineering

Systems of equations describe forces, currents, and quantities that must satisfy relationships.

Images

An image is a grid of pixel values that can be represented by a matrix and approximated using fewer numbers.

Shared pattern: Represent with numbers, calculate, interpret in context.
