Dot products: compare vectors

A dot product combines two vectors into one number and helps measure length and direction.

Calculate a dot product

(2,1)·(3,4)=2·3+1·4=10

Find length

For v=(3,4): ‖v‖=√(v·v)=√(3²+4²)=5.

Perpendicular vectors

Two nonzero vectors are perpendicular when their dot product is zero. Example: (1,2)·(2,−1)=0.

Dot products help compare directions and calculate angles.
Try it: (1,3)·(4,2)

Answer: 10
