Matrices as transformations

A matrix acts like a machine that changes vectors consistently.

A simple stretch

Let A=[[2,0],[0,1]]. It doubles the horizontal coordinate and leaves the vertical one alone.

A(1,3)=(2,3)

Columns show what happens

The basic directions are (1,0) and (0,1). Applying A gives its columns: A(1,0)=(2,0), A(0,1)=(0,1). These describe how every vector moves.

Combine changes

Matrix multiplication combines transformations. The order matters.

Try it: [[3,0],[0,1]](2,4)

Answer: (6,4)
