Eigenvalues and eigenvectors

An eigenvector keeps its direction under a matrix. Its eigenvalue tells how it is scaled.

The key equation

Av=λv

v is a nonzero eigenvector and λ its eigenvalue. The matrix may stretch or shrink v, but it stays on the same line.

Example

For A=[[2,0],[0,3]], A(1,0)=(2,0)=2(1,0); so (1,0) has eigenvalue 2.

How to find them

Rewrite as (A−λI)v=0. A nonzero solution exists when det(A−λI)=0. Solve for λ, then find the matching vectors.

Eigenvectors reveal stable directions and repeated patterns in data.
Try it: If A(1,0)=(6,0), what is its eigenvalue?

Answer: 6
