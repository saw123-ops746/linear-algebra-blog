Matrices: tables that organize numbers

Matrices store numbers and describe rules acting on vectors.

Rows and columns

This matrix has 2 rows and 2 columns:

A = [[1,2],[3,4]]

Add same-size matrices

[[1,2],[3,4]]+[[5,1],[2,0]]=[[6,3],[5,4]]

Multiply a matrix by a vector

[[1,2],[3,4]][2,1]=[1·2+2·1,3·2+4·1]=[4,10]
Dimensions: A 2×3 matrix can multiply a 3×1 vector; the answer is 2×1.
Try it: [[2,0],[1,3]][4,2]

Answer: [8,10]
