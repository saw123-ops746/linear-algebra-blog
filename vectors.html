Vectors: numbers with meaning

Vectors describe points, movement, and measurements.

A vector is an ordered list

The vector (3, 2) has two components. On a map it could mean 3 units east and 2 units north. Vectors can also hold data, such as height and hours studied.

Add matching components

(3,2)+(1,4)=(4,6)

Multiply every component to scale a vector: 2(3,2)=(6,4).

Key idea: Order matters: (3,2) is different from (2,3).
Try it: (2,1)+(3,4)

Answer: (5,5)
