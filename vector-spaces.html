Span, independence, and basis

These ideas describe which directions vectors can build and how many independent directions a space has.

Span

The span is all vectors you can make by scaling and adding a set. In the plane, (1,0) and (0,1) span every (x,y), since x(1,0)+y(0,1)=(x,y).

Independence

Vectors are independent if none can be made from the others. (1,0),(0,1) are independent; (1,0),(2,0) are dependent.

Basis and dimension

A basis is independent and spans the space. The plane has two basis directions, so its dimension is 2.

Remember: A basis must span and be independent.
Try it: Are (1,0) and (0,1) a basis for the plane?

Answer: Yes; they span every point and are independent.
